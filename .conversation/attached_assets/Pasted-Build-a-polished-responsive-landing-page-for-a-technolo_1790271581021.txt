Build a polished, responsive landing page for a technology startup called “LabLens AI.”

STARTUP OVERVIEW
LabLens AI is an AI-assisted laboratory record-continuity platform for graduate student researchers in research-intensive academic laboratories.

Our target users are graduate students who run multiple experiments and already maintain complicated workarounds across physical notebooks, electronic notebooks, phone photos, tablets, instrument computers, PowerPoint, Google Drive, coded folders, and tutorials.

LabLens AI should be presented as a B2C technology startup. The graduate student researcher is the primary user and potential subscriber. Do not show pricing yet because willingness to pay has not been validated. Focus on joining the beta or waitlist.

CORE PROBLEM
Graduate researchers often have to manually reconstruct the context of an experiment from scattered procedures, observations, images, spectra, instrument files, slides, and folders.

CORE BENEFIT
Fast recovery of the complete context of an experiment without manually rebuilding information from scattered records.

POSITIONING
For graduate students in research-intensive academic labs who maintain multi-tool record workarounds and are dissatisfied with manually rebuilding experiment context from scattered records, LabLens AI is an AI-assisted laboratory record-continuity platform that provides fast recovery of complete experiment context.

Unlike the current patchwork of notebooks, shared drives, instrument computers, electronic lab notebooks, and presentation files, LabLens AI organizes every experiment around one connected record linking procedures, observations, images, analysis, and instrument files.

3. HOW LABLENS WORKS
Create four steps:

Capture
Upload or link procedures, notes, observations, images, spectra, spreadsheets, slides, and instrument files.

Connect
Organize everything around a single experiment record using project names, experiment codes, compounds, dates, and file relationships.

Recover
Search by experiment, compound, procedure, keyword, instrument, date, or result and quickly recover the full context.

Continue
Generate a source-linked experiment summary or advisor update so the researcher can continue work without manually rebuilding the record.
