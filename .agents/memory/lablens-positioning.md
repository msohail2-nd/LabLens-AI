---
name: LabLens AI positioning
description: The product positioning and current public-site capability boundaries for LabLens AI.
---

LabLens AI is positioned as an AI-powered research continuity and knowledge-retrieval platform that complements existing LIMS and electronic lab notebooks rather than replacing them. The public site currently offers beta interest signup only; cross-tool search, evidence linking, experiment reconstruction, and AI summaries are planned capabilities, not live product features.

**Why:** The founders want a credible distinction from established LIMS/ELN products without making categorical superiority claims or implying planned features already work.

**How to apply:** Keep future public copy clear about the complementary role and planned status of product features; do not imply current file uploads, integrations, AI search, or experiment workspace.
